# Content guide

Business OS: Digital Clinic communicates like a calm technical partner: specific, diagnostic, and honest about uncertainty. The product is not a generic developer portfolio and does not use inflated AI marketing language.

## Core message

> Диагностирую цифровые проблемы бизнеса и превращаю их в быстрые, удобные и защищённые IT-системы.

Supporting copy should explain the business process, user friction, technical intervention, or observable signal behind a claim.

## Voice

- Clear and professional, without bureaucratic filler.
- Confident about implemented capabilities; explicit about planned or preliminary work.
- Focused on business symptoms before tools and frameworks.
- Written primarily in Russian with short established English system labels where they improve scanning.
- Respectful of the reader's time: short headings, concrete paragraphs, and descriptive calls to action.

## Claims and evidence

- Never invent clients, testimonials, revenue, conversion, time savings, uptime, or security outcomes.
- A metric may be presented as real only when its source, period, and calculation are available.
- Use `Concept Project`, `Educational Project`, `Personal Project`, `Demo Case`, or `NDA Case` labels exactly where applicable.
- Mark simulations with the visible phrase `Демонстрационная симуляция`.
- Preliminary diagnostic output must state that it does not replace a complete audit.
- Do not promise absolute security, complete anti-copying protection, or Java-only DDoS prevention.

## Homepage rules

- The hero states the business outcome, not the technology stack.
- Business Health starts as `Не измерено` until the user supplies diagnostic data.
- Before/after vitals describe a target process and never imply a completed client result.
- Featured case teasers must retain their project-type label when reused elsewhere.
- Project details must include a visible limitation and phrase proposed metrics as future verification signals, never completed results.
- The security section explains layered risk reduction and the role of CDN, WAF, monitoring, backups, and origin protection.
- Calls to action must lead to an implemented destination. Planned interactive modules are named as planned and do not collect data early.

## Russian editorial style

- Prefer active verbs: `диагностирую`, `проектирую`, `проверяю`, `измеряю`.
- Use `ё` consistently in user-facing Russian copy.
- Avoid unnecessary exclamation marks, emoji, clickbait, and vague superlatives.
- Use an em dash for explanatory pauses and non-breaking meaning where typography permits.
- Keep headings sentence-cased unless a short system label intentionally uses uppercase.

## Review checklist

Before publishing new content, verify that:

1. Every result claim has evidence or an explicit demo/preliminary label.
2. The text describes the user's problem before proposing a technology.
3. Security language describes risk reduction rather than guarantees.
4. Links and buttons lead to implemented destinations.
5. No personal data, client identity, private media, or NDA detail is exposed.
